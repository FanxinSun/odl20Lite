**Statistics, each naming its formula (plan §4 rule 3).**
- Orbit residual of a fit: **`S_N = sqrt( (1/N) Σ_{i=1..N} ‖q_i(θ̂) − p_i‖² )`**, `N` = the number of epochs (96); **`S_{N−p} = sqrt( (1/(N−p)) Σ ‖q_i − p_i‖² )`** with `p` the number of fitted parameters (7; 15 for ECOM2); `S_N/√3`; the radial / along-track / cross-track RMS (each `sqrt(mean of that component²)`); the largest epoch residual. Recovered effective area-to-mass: **`A·C_R/m = ĉ · (A_cb · C_R,cb / m_sv)`** (`m²/kg`), `ĉ` the fitted `srp_scale`, `A_cb`, `C_R,cb`, `m_sv` the cannonball template's values. *(See the open point below.)*
- Block statistics (`B-*`), `n_b >= 2` required: **mean `m_b = (1/n_b) Σ x_i`; Bessel standard deviation `s_b = sqrt( (1/(n_b − 1)) Σ (x_i − m_b)² )`.** Two separations are reported side by side, as the frozen rows are: **`B-*-pooled`: `d_pooled = |m_1 − m_2| / sqrt( ((n_1 − 1) s_1² + (n_2 − 1) s_2²) / (n_1 + n_2 − 2) )`**; **`B-*-sem`: `t_sem = |m_1 − m_2| / sqrt( s_1²/n_1 + s_2²/n_2 )`**.
- ECOM2 `E-*` (`d4b1_order()`: nine SRP parameters `D0 D2c D2s D4c D4s Y0 B0 B1c B1s` plus six state = 15): per satellite `S_N`, `S_{N−15}`, and the eclipse status `min ν` over its 96 epochs; **both** the all-31 and the eclipse-free (`min ν = 1`) `median(S_N)` (`E-01`) and `max(S_N)` (`E-02`) are reported. The median of an even count is the mean of the two middle values.
