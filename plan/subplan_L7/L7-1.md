# L7 step 1 — The whole force model through the registry

Step 1 of layer **L7** in [`../PLAN.md`](../PLAN.md) §3.8. Status: **DONE**.
Paths are relative to `rewrite/`, the rewrite's tree, as they are in the plan.

---

1. **DONE** — L2's acceleration-producing models — the gravity field, the third bodies, the tides,
   and any other that L2 gated — as `dyn::Force` plugins, so that `DYN-R-023`'s registry carries
   the whole force model an arc fit propagates. Gate: through the registry, each plugin reproduces
   its L2 module's own gated values at stated points, with what a wrapper adds — the frame rotation
   between the state's frame and the model's, and the km↔m crossing — checked independently of the
   wrapper, and its state Jacobian against finite differences. The gravity field's truncation is
   chosen here, from its size degree by degree at each of L4's reference points against the
   smallest force the model keeps — L4's ranking table carries the field only as J2, which ranks it
   but cannot say where to cut it.

   *Added 2026-09-24, found at L4's exit review.* L3 froze the plugin surface after L2 existed,
   and only L4's five forces were ever written as plugins; L2's models are evaluated directly
   (`tests/l2_floors.cpp`), and no step wrapped them. Every fit in this layer propagates the whole
   model, so the wrapping comes before the estimator. It is placed here rather than in L4, whose
   scope is the non-gravitational forces, because this is the first layer that needs it — and it
   is written as a step, not as a note, because the ranking table showed what happens to an
   obligation no step carries.

   *Rule-4 report and rulings, 2026-10-06.* The surface takes a GCRS position in metres and
   returns an acceleration with its analytic Jacobians. No L2 model is yet a plugin; the full
   field's gradient was decided at L3 and never built; and a tide has no way to become an
   acceleration, since `CoefficientSet` cannot be assembled from increments and nothing synthesises
   them. **The plugins** — one gravity field, one third body per body, one relativity term per
   plugin, and one tide plugin, because the increments' sum is formed once and applied once — live
   in a module of their own, apart from the estimator, which depends only on the force interface.
   **Two additive entry points in `gravity`**, under `DYN-Q-001`'s terms: the field's gradient by
   the recursion decided at L3, and the synthesis of an increment set. Each is checked by
   identities the field itself must satisfy — Laplace's trace, symmetry, the closed-form J₂ and
   point-mass tensors, the Legendre equation, linearity — not by its own arithmetic. **L4's ranking
   table is corrected first** (§3.5's correction note). **The truncation** is the lowest degree at
   which the RMS of the discarded acceleration over the sphere is at most the smallest other force
   registered at that point, measured through the registry in the same run — a comparator the
   field's degree cannot move. It is kept as a function, with its table at L4's four points; each
   row names the forces it compared against, and the maximum along the test orbit is reported
   beside the RMS. **The Jacobians' gate is L6's**: a third-derivative bound that is a bound,
   however loose, with its looseness and the power it costs stated; a noise term measured against
   an extended-precision evaluation whose own error is stated, over the whole stencil, and asserted
   point by point; and each control's power written down at its own geometry before the run. L4's
   plugins hold one Earth-orientation record, so the exit gate's fits state each plugin's handling
   and its size over the arc, and multi-day arcs are carried. **The exit gate's statistic** is the
   3-D RMS over the IGS file's own epochs: the revival's script prints a "position RMS" whose
   square is the sum of its printed per-axis squares, so the frozen numbers are 3-D. Their
   denominator, N or N − 7, does not show in the printed output, so a fit that reaches its number
   only under N is reported as such and brought to the manager unchanged.

   *Closed 2026-10-06.* Built in the order ruled, each check registered before it ran and run once.

   matches its definition to 4.9–58 ε over 145 cases, Laplace's trace vanishes to 2.7 × 10⁻¹⁵, and
   nothing already shipped moved by an ulp. Through the registry every plugin reproduces its L2
   module, with the wrapper's rotation and unit crossing checked without the wrapper. The
   Jacobians' gate held with every noise term asserted point by point, the largest deviation 0.073
   of its bound, so L6's failure — a noise source the frozen term lacked — did not recur. The

   720 km and 297 at 300 km. **For the exit gate's GPS fits** the force set is the Sun, the Moon,
   the tides and the three relativity terms, and the field's degree is the table's 10: the tree's
   own instrument decides, with TN36's twelve printed beside it. The planets are an option whose
   effect is predicted before a fit and printed after it, because a registered planet's force on
