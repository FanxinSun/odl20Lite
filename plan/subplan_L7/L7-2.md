2. **TODO** — Batch least squares with normal equations **scaled by default**, never as an
   option. The predecessor's columns spanned twelve orders of magnitude and its unscaled
   inverse was noise that presented as unobservability.

   *Rule-4 report and rulings, 2026-10-06 (steps 2–5 reported together).* Demmel's LAPACK Working
   Note 14 shows that the unscaled Cholesky solution already meets the scaled error bound —
   "nothing is gained by scaling" the solve — and the executor measured it on NIST's eleven
   certified linear datasets: scaling by exact powers of two gives a bit-identical solution. So the
   reason above stands corrected, not withdrawn. An unscaled inverse cannot make noise of
   Cholesky's accuracy; it makes noise of every decision read from the matrix — a condition number,
   an observability rule, a damping, a tolerance, a correlation — each of which moves by orders of
   magnitude with a parameter's unit. **Scaling stays the default and the only path**: symmetric
   equilibration of the total normal matrix by exact powers of two, after all information is added,
   undone exactly, with no unscaled path in the interface. **A refusal names the parameter and
   why**: no information, a scaled factorisation that breaks down, or a derived error bound that
   certifies no digit. It carries the scaled condition number, each parameter's own (`Nᵢᵢ (N⁻¹)ᵢᵢ`,
   unit-free, as GEODYN prints it) and the weakest direction. Its one hatch is elimination of named
   parameters at stated values, logged — the operation a hard constraint is — not a truncated
   minimum-norm solution, which would return numbers for combinations the data cannot see. **The
   gate is four layers, each registered before it runs**: the forming error against exact products;
   the scaled backward error with an exact residual, a bound with no condition number in it; NIST's
   certified estimates and standard deviations, with Filip — certified, yet beyond normal equations
   in double — refused as registered; and unit invariance, with a decision rule on the unscaled
   matrix computed in the test and shown calling Pontius rank-deficient where the scaled one reaches
   its certified digits. The module has kernels of its own and no new dependency, and runs in
   double precision in production.
