5. **TODO** — Joint covariance over the state and every parameter registered in L3. Gate: a
   joint confidence region for two correlated parameters recovered from one fit — the thing the
   predecessor could only approach by grid-profiling one against the other.

   *Ruled 2026-10-06, on the rule-4 report.* The joint covariance over the state and every
   registered parameter comes from the scaled inverse, unscaled exactly, and the correlations from
   its own diagonal, unit-free. The two-parameter region uses the closed forms — χ² with two degrees
   of freedom when σ₀ is known, Fisher's F when it is estimated — which reproduce 298 of 300
   printed F values and every printed χ² value, the two misses being the table's. No retrievable
   source prints a worked joint region of a fit, so the region's evidence is independent of the
   covariance: the grid profile of the objective — the predecessor's own method, now the
   independent reference — exact for NIST's linear sets and within a nonlinearity bound registered
   first for an orbit fit; the exit gate's finite-difference correlations; and NIST's certified
   standard deviations for the diagonal. The formal covariance and the one scaled by the fit's own
   variance are both reported, never one for the other.
