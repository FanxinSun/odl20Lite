4. **TODO** — Altimetry: Jason from the CNES box-wing macromodel (Cerri et al. 2010),
   Sentinel-6 from the ESA/EUMETSAT metadata.

   *Opened 2026-09-24.* The rule-4 and licence search covers each mission's data **and its
   attitude law**, reported before anything is built. No campaign consumes these missions — L8's
   laser-ranging campaign is LightSail-2 — so each law is built with step 6's guards and a
   real-data control where its source is clean and complete, or carried with the attitude
   refusing, as step 3 ruled for its constellations.

   *Rule-4 and licence search, 2026-09-24.* Cerri et al. 2010 is paywalled; the working source is
   its lead author's own CNES technical note on the DORIS satellites' models, published for
   "external diffusion" on the International DORIS Service's site — whose site-wide legal notice
   is an unfilled placeholder — printing the box-wing values directly. It gives Jason-2 and -3 a
   shared macromodel whose optics sum to one, mass and centre of mass as a baseline plus an
   operational offset file, and the TOPEX attitude law — yaw steering, and fixed yaw below about
   15° of β, with ramps and flips whose timing is operational, not closed-form. Jason-1's model is
   empirically tuned: its optics do not sum to one and it carries a 0.97 scale factor. A second
   note gives Sentinel-6 twelve flat plates summing to one and a complete closed-form attitude law
   — nadir pointing with small roll, pitch and yaw oscillations in the argument of latitude — and
   the macromodel note's appendix prints a numerical SRP example.

   *Ruled the same day.* The CNES notes are used under the `RS14` ruling — published physical
   values, each cited, the edition pinned, the document's own statement of external diffusion and
   the placeholder notice both recorded. **Sentinel-6 is built**, and its appendix example is
   reproduced: the first published case in this tree that runs a real macromodel through the
   photon-pressure kernel (§4 rule 2). Its attitude law is built from the note's own definitions of
   its angles, axes and rotation order, quoted, with the argument of latitude's direction checked
   along a propagated trajectory, as μ's now is. **Jason-2 and -3 are built**: yaw steering and
   fixed yaw, the switch at the printed β marked approximate and the ramps and flips unmodelled,
   so the attitude is discontinuous there — recorded as an integrator event, II/IIA's precedent;
   mass and centre of mass by epoch from the offset file if it is openly retrievable on the same
   terms, and otherwise the baseline under an explicitly named selector, the absence recorded.
   **Jason-1 is carried**, the library refusing: tuned coefficients that are not physical fractions
   are not what the schema holds, and nothing consumes it. Each built law carries step 6's guards
   and a real-data control if open attitude data exists, its absence recorded if not.

   *Reviewed 2026-09-24, after the build.* Sentinel-6 and Jason-2 and -3 are built and Jason-1
   refuses. **The appendix example is SPOT-5's, and it found the kernel's scope.** The general
   formula, transcribed afresh, reproduces its 20 printed vectors; the photon-pressure kernel
   writes the absorbed and diffuse terms as 1 − ρ, which equals the formula's α + δ only when the
   three coefficients sum to one — true of every model built, false of SPOT-5's table, where the
   kernel is 49% off — and the kernel equals the formula exactly for Sentinel-6's energy-conserving
   model at 40 geometries. So the kernel is validated through the formula within its scope, and
   its scope is now written into its specification and guarded by the schema's refusal of
   coefficients that do not sum to one, shown firing, with both checks standing tests. **Three
   things remain.** *The real-data control is run:* open Jason attitude quaternions and orbits were
   found, and Jason's frame and yaw-steering sign are derived, not confirmed — the class of
   convention every GPS defect belonged to, and real data found them — so one registered Jason-3
   comparison is made, with the quaternion convention settled by the nadir test as for CODE's
   files; Sentinel-6's listing is tried once by direct path, its absence recorded if it fails, and
   its frame stays a marked assumption until then. *The reason for Jason's baseline mass is stated
   truly:* the offset log is a time series a lookup could serve, and it is deferred because
   nothing consumes Jason, with the file's location recorded.
