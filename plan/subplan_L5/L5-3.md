3. **TODO** — GLONASS, BeiDou and QZSS, from the IAC metadata, CSNO 2019 and the Cabinet Office
   release, consolidated through the IGS satellite metadata SINEX.

   *Opened 2026-09-24.* The rule-4 and licence search covers each constellation's data **and its
   attitude law** — carried since L4 step 6, with GLONASS-M's in Dilssner et al. 2011 and BeiDou's
   in the CSNO standard — and is reported before anything is built. No campaign consumes these
   constellations (L8's GNSS campaign is the three GPS baselines), so after the report each law is
   either built, with step 6's guards and a real-data control as Galileo's were, or carried with
   the attitude refusing wherever the ideal law does not hold, never silently wrong.

   *Rule-4 and licence search, 2026-09-24.* **GLONASS:** no operator metadata; `RS14` prints
   box-wing tables for GLONASS, GLONASS-M and GLONASS-K, the last with dimensions from a personal
   communication; the attitude law is Dilssner et al. 2011, freely hosted. **BeiDou:** the CSNO
   standard prints data and attitude in one document, but no centre of mass, curved surfaces the
   schema cannot hold, a manoeuvre-yaw mode whose extracted equations are garbled, and no stated
   redistribution terms. **QZSS:** the Cabinet Office's per-satellite documents print frame, both
   attitude modes, mass at beginning and end of life, geometry and optics labelled in words, and
   are "freely available to any user" with credit.

   *Ruled the same day, constellation by constellation.* **GLONASS is built:** `RS14`'s values
   under the GPS ruling, GLONASS-K's chain recorded where it ends as the IIF's is; and Dilssner's
   law from the source's own words for each turn, not assumed to be new constants in Kouba's — the
   IIF's night turn was a different mechanism from its noon turn, and GLONASS-M's midnight turn,
   rotating at full rate from shadow entry and holding once it reaches the exit yaw, may be too.
   **QZSS is built:** the frame mapped and tested by the property the document states, the Sun in
   the −X hemisphere, since no paired coordinates are printed; mass chosen explicitly at beginning
   or end of life; yaw steering as a parameterisation of the nominal law; and orbit-normal mode as
   a new, general mechanism, its switch at the printed |β| marked approximate, since the real
   switch is commanded, with the real-data control showing where it fell. **BeiDou is carried**,
   and the library refuses it: four open items — surfaces the schema cannot hold, no centre of
   mass, unread manoeuvre equations, unstated terms — and no consumer, and a schema is not
   extended for a client that does not yet exist. Each built law carries step 6's two guards and a
   real-data control, as Galileo's did.

   *Reviewed 2026-09-24, after the build.* GLONASS and QZSS are built as ruled and BeiDou refuses
   with its reasons tested; `ci.sh` exits 0 on `5ec89a1`, 13 gates, 368 tests, 708 artefacts
   byte-identical, verified by the manager. GLONASS-M's midnight turn was confirmed, against the
   paper's own satellite data before it was built, to be a different mechanism from its noon turn —
   a ramp that holds — and both match CODE's attitude within 0.03°; QZSS's frame mapping matches
   CODE's attitude for two QZSS satellites within 0.0002°. The CSNO standard turns out to be a
   file format holding no satellite values at all, which confirms BeiDou's carry. **Two things
   remain.** *QZSS's orbit-normal mode has no real-data control* — four sampled days is not a
   search — so QZS-1's β is scanned day by day over the span CODE's attitude archive covers while
   it flew, the control is run in any low-β window found, and if none exists the absence is
   recorded with the scan; the basis for applying a mapping checked on other QZSS satellites to
   QZS-1 is stated from the sources. *GLONASS's cylindrical bus faces are built as flat plates*,
   stated in their citations, and that stays — with the error bounded by a number, the flat law
   against `RS14`'s blend at stated geometry as a fraction of the whole force. Curved surfaces in
   the schema become one carried question, GLONASS's and BeiDou's together, decided when a
   consumer needs them and gated on their own, since the photon-pressure kernel was gated for flat
   panels. QZS-2, -3, -4 and -1R, and the omitted antenna cover, are carried.
