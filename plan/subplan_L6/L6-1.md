1. **TODO** — Formats: SP3, TLE, CRD and CPF, the optical observation formats, SINEX and ANTEX.
   Each reader refuses a field it does not recognise rather than defaulting it. The predecessor
   read an SP3 interval from the wrong field and skipped a fixed header length; both are
   acceptance tests here.

   *Ruled 2026-09-25, after the first build.* The readers and writers are built from their pinned
   specifications, the predecessor's two SP3 defects fail and are designed out, round-tripping
   found three writer defects, and the comparison tools read SP3 through this module. **Step 1 also
   carries SP3 interpolation.** The three defects of the comparison tools lived in their own
   position lookup — the nearest sample, elapsed time for a calendar date, a frame assumed — and
   they still interpolate each in their own way, so one tree-wide facility closes that: an SP3
   ephemeris interpolated by a stated order, the order's reason given, refusing outside the
   sampled span and across a gap or a flagged manoeuvre, its accuracy checked by holding samples
   out and interpolating them from their neighbours, and the tools re-pointed at it. It is data
   access, not a measurement model, so it belongs here rather than to `measmod`, which uses it.
   The Galileo control is re-run through it, since linear interpolation was one named suspect for
   its unexplained residual (`GALY-Q-002`).
