4. **DONE** — `measmod`: ephemeris-position, SLR range and optical angles against one
   interface, with the station and site registry. Light time, tropospheric refraction and the
   observer's own motion belong to the model, not to the caller. Gate: each measurement's
   partials against finite differences, plus a published SLR range case.

   *Rule-4 report and rulings, 2026-10-06.* **No published end-to-end SLR range case exists in
   anything retrievable without an account**: the IERS routines print term-level cases only, the
   ILRS benchmark was never published, and the textbooks' worked examples cannot be obtained; the
   search is recorded (§4 rule 4). So the gate is restated as what the sources support. **G1**:
   every observation type's partials against finite differences in `STM-R-004`/`-005`'s form —
   several perturbation sizes, their spread the band, the band within an order of magnitude of a
   sizing written before the first run, the light time iterated to its fixed point. **G2**: the
   three published IERS cases — the FCULa and FCULb mapping functions exact, the Mendes–Pavlis
   zenith delay to 1 mm, the source's stated accuracy, with the 3.8 µm by which the printed
   equations miss the printed number reported beside it and not pursued, since finding it would
   mean reading the routine. **G3**: a closed-form two-way light time, target and station both in
   uniform motion, for both epoch events. **G4**: the Shapiro term against an independent
   evaluation, labelled not published. **G5**: one real LAGEOS pass against the ILRS orbit product
   of its week, its envelope — every omitted term sized from a source or from first principles —
   committed before the comparison, compared once, no bias removed; inside passes, outside is a
   finding the step does not close on. Printed test values are published observations, used with
   the routine named and cited; no routine text enters the tree. **Station displacement is out of
   this step and of the MVP**: no gate through L8 needs it — L8 step 2's frozen residual is 57 m —
   so the omission is written, with its magnitudes, into the specification and into every modelled
   range's record of what was applied; the solid Earth tide (four published cases) and the pole
   tide are a named follow-on, and ocean loading's coefficients come only from a request-form
   service. The station registry is SLRF2020 (release 2026.02.05) with the ILRS eccentricities in
   up/north/east, pinned under the ILRS's own statement that its products are not copyrighted; it
   refuses an unknown station, an epoch outside an eccentricity's span and a site after a
   post-seismic event. Optical sites come from the caller with a citation, the only public list
   being GPL-derived and carrying observers' names. **An angle observation declares its reduction,
   with no default**: a place reduced against catalogue stars differs from the satellite's GCRS
   direction by up to 20.5″, the stars' annual aberration, which the satellite does not share.
   Partials are complete rows with respect to the target's state at the bounce or emission epoch,
   the light time's dependence on the trajectory folded in, so L7 maps them with the transition
   matrix and adds nothing. The centre of mass is a cited input for a spherical target; a
   non-spherical one is refused until a campaign needs its array's offset. The order: the
   specification, with every gate and refusal written; the registry; the troposphere and Shapiro
   terms; the range model; ephemeris position; angles; the real pass.

   *Ruled 2026-10-06, on the range gate's first run.* Run once by its frozen sizing, the
   finite-difference gate met its band and its velocity columns but missed its per-size bound in
   seven of eight cases, and the cause was the sizing, not the model. It left out the noise of the
   Earth-rotation angle, formed unreduced near 171 rad, whose last bit moves a station 0.16 µm —
   predicted from the formula's documentation before it was measured at 0.161 µm — and it took the
   range's third derivative at the stencil's centre and from the geometry alone, where a bound must
   hold over the whole stencil and the troposphere adds 7 × 10⁻⁴ of it. The first run stays
   recorded as run. The sizing is amended, dated and labelled as made after the miss, by those two
   terms only — the noise floor by the frozen sizing's own three-ulp convention, the derivative as a
   bound over the stencil with every modelled term — and the gate runs once more in two
   configurations, both asserted: the real chain, which is the model L7 will use, and an exact rigid
   rotation, which is the configuration the frozen noise term describes. The wrong rows must still
   fail both. The angle gate's sizing takes the same two terms before it first runs. A different
   geometry chosen after a miss is not open.

   *Checkpoint 2026-10-06: items 1–4 done.* The specification; the registry, whose 186 pads are
   the header's 184 plus two added after it was written; the troposphere and Shapiro terms with G2
   and G4; and the range model with G3 and G1. Under Amendment A1, committed before its run
   (`2290f18`), the range gate holds in both configurations — at worst 0.995 of its bound on the
   real chain and 0.998 rigid, a bound that is not slack — and every wrong row fails it, the least
   by 193 times. The chain term enters the angle gate as zero, since no perturbation of the target
   moves the observation epoch at which the station and the Earth are evaluated, so that gate's two
   configurations must agree to arithmetic noise. Verified by the manager from an isolated export
   of the commit: `ci.sh` exits 0 on `040cb44`, 13 gates, 512 tests, 758 artefacts byte-identical.

   *Ruled 2026-10-06, on the angle gate's one run.* Its criteria held in every case and in both
   configurations, the configurations agreed, and four of its five frozen controls failed their
   wrong rows by 3.5 × 10⁴ to 4 × 10⁶ times the bound. The fifth, a row without the diurnal
   aberration's Jacobian, failed by 0.47 times it, not the 100 asked: the rule's geometry looks
   north while the station moves east, and the Jacobian's first-order effect is the line-of-sight
   component of that motion, here zero. The control was sized without being evaluated at the
   geometry it would run at. The run stands as run and the geometry is not re-selected. The
   control's claim moves to a new check, registered before it runs, at a closed-form geometry with
   the station moving along the line of sight, where a row without the Jacobian and a row with it
   reversed must each fail by a margin predicted in advance. From here on, every check's power
   against the defect it exists to catch is evaluated at its own geometry before it runs, G5's
   envelope included.

   *Checkpoint 2026-10-06: items 5 and 6 done.* Ephemeris position, its gate held on its one run,

   *Ruled 2026-10-06, on G5's station, from metadata alone and before any residual exists.*
   Yarragadee, not Matera. Read literally, the selection rule picked Matera, the one station with no
   Data Handling File record for the week — and its eccentricity is zero by construction, so G5 would
   be blind there to an eccentricity applied wrongly or not at all, one of the defects it exists to
   catch. The rule's "no Data Handling File entry" meant no record that excludes, quarantines or
   materially biases the data. Yarragadee's only record is a long-term mean range bias of
   4.8 ± 3.8 mm, which enters the envelope as an unsigned term, and its 3.18 m eccentricity,
   determined six times with millimetre scatter, gives G5 that power.
