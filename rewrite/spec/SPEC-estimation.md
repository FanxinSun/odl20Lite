## 1. Purpose and scope

L7 turns the forces, the integrators, the variational equations (L3) and the measurement models (L6) into **fits**: the whole force model carried by the `dyn::Force` registry (step 1), batch least squares with the normal equations **scaled by default** (step 2), Levenberg–Marquardt (step 3), a priori constraints, isotropic and RTN (step 4), and the **joint covariance** over the state and every registered parameter (step 5). The exit gate (plan §3.8, amended 2026-09-24): *a fit over real data reaches its frozen residual and reports a joint covariance whose correlations are reproduced by finite differences*; cannonball fits with L4's forces reach the `G-*` frozen residuals with their force parameters stated in the test, and a box-wing fit reading L5's library reaches them too.

**Not in scope:** the campaigns (L8); a sail's own macromodel (§3.9); station displacement (outside the MVP, `SPEC-measmod`); the NRLMSISE-00 altitude cutoffs and the attitude discontinuities as integrator events are **carried into** this layer (plan §3.8) and are decided at the step that fits an arc through them.

**Stubs, and when each is written:** §3 (definitions) and §4 (required behaviour) per step, as each step opens, after its rule-4 report is ruled on; §5 (interfaces), §6 (precision), §7 (refusals) likewise; §9 (provenance) at each close. *(v0.2.)* **Step 1 is not specified here.** Ruling R1 (2026-10-06) puts the force-model plugins in a module of their own, `forcemodel` (`SPEC-forcemodel.md`, Spec ID `FMOD`), because the estimator of steps 2 – 5 depends only on `dyn`'s `Force` interface and not on `gravity`, `tides`, `thirdbody` or `relativity`; this document's §3 – §7 are written for steps 2 – 5 when each opens, and its §8.1 is the exit gate's, which spans all five.

## 3. Definitions and conventions

*(Step 2, v0.3.)* The exit gate's statistic is defined in §8.1 because that is where it is registered. What follows is the batch estimator of step 2. **Derivation declaration:** written from `DEM89`, `LAPK`, `NISTL`, `GEO1`, `BRN52`, `MINP` and from no implementation of any estimator; the predecessor's code was not opened.

**3.1 The problem.** A linearised weighted least-squares problem over `n` named **parameters**: `m` scalar **observation rows** `(aₖ, rₖ, σₖ)`, `aₖ ∈ ℝⁿ` the partials of the modelled observation with respect to the parameters at the linearisation point, `rₖ` the **residual** (observed minus modelled) and `σₖ > 0` its standard deviation, in the residual's own units. The estimator returns the **correction** `x̂` that minimises `Ω(x) = Σₖ ((rₖ − aₖᵀx)/σₖ)²` (adding it to the linearisation point is the caller's). Rows are **whitened** on entry: `ãₖ = fl(aₖ/σₖ)` componentwise, `r̃ₖ = fl(rₖ/σₖ)` (σₖ = 1 divides exactly). **The whitened doubles are the data**: every bound below is relative to the exact solution of the problem whose rows are those doubles.

**3.2 The normal equations.** `N = Σₖ ãₖãₖᵀ`, `b = Σₖ ãₖr̃ₖ`, formed as `EST-R-203` states. `D = diag(√Nᵢᵢ)`, `A = D⁻¹ND⁻¹` (unit diagonal, the matrix of the cosines between the whitened columns). **The scaled error** of a computed solution `x̂` against the exact `x = N⁻¹b` is `φ(x̂, x) = ‖D(x̂ − x)‖₂ / ‖Dx̂‖₂`: **invariant under any change of the parameters' units** (a positive diagonal rescaling of the columns changes `D` and `x` inversely), which is the reason it is the measure of this specification and a relative error of the unscaled vector is not (`DEM89`: small components of `x` are computed to the same relative accuracy as large ones when `κ(A)` is moderate).

**3.3 The scaling (the manager's ruling R6).** For a free parameter `i`, `Nᵢᵢ = m·2ᵏ` with `m ∈ [½, 1)` (`frexp`), the **scale exponent** is `eᵢ = ⌊k/2⌋` (`EST-R-201`), `S = diag(2^(−eᵢ))`, `N_s = S N S`, `b_s = S b`, `y = S⁻¹x`, `Q_s = N_s⁻¹`, `Q = N⁻¹ = S Q_s S`. **Scaling by exact powers of two is exact** (barring over- and underflow, `LAPK`): `N_s` carries no rounding the scaling added, and the scaled diagonal `Nᵢᵢ 2^(−2eᵢ)` lies in `[½, 2)`, so `κ₂(N_s) ≤ 4 κ₂(D⁻¹ND⁻¹)` and `N_s` is within `4n` of the best diagonal scaling (`DEM89`'s statement of Van der Sluis). **Consequence, stated so that nobody expects otherwise** (the manager's correction of the premise of this step, 2026-10-06): the scaling cannot change the Cholesky solution — `DEM89` Theorem 2.1 proves that the unscaled factorisation already meets the scaled bound, and with powers of two the two are *bit for bit the same computation* (measured on the eleven NIST datasets, PROVENANCE §40.7). What it changes is **every decision read from the matrix**: the condition number, the observability, the refusals, the per-parameter diagnostics, the correlations, and (step 3) the damping and the tolerances — each of which moves by orders of magnitude with a parameter's unit when read from the unscaled matrix and does not when read from `N_s`.

**3.4 The error model.** `u = 2⁻⁵³`, the unit roundoff of IEEE binary64 under round-to-nearest; `γₖ = ku/(1 − ku)`; `χₙ = 1/(1 − (n+1)u)`. The constants below are `DEM89`'s: Lemma 2.1 (`|(L̂L̂ᵀ − H)ᵢⱼ| ≤ (n+1)u χₙ √(HᵢᵢHⱼⱼ)`), the substitutions' `|δL| ≤ nu|L̂|` that its proof uses, and the proof's final line, `‖D⁻¹FD⁻¹‖₂ ≤ nη + χₙ[(3n²+n)u + n³u²]`, `η` the relative size of the initial error of the matrix factored. For the forming of `N` the elementary dot-product bound `|fl(Σₖ xₖyₖ) − Σₖ xₖyₖ| ≤ γₘ Σₖ|xₖyₖ|` and Cauchy–Schwarz (`Σₖ|ãₖᵢãₖⱼ| ≤ √(NᵢᵢNⱼⱼ)`) give a **componentwise-scaled** error, `|N̂ᵢⱼ − Nᵢⱼ| ≤ γₘ√(NᵢᵢNⱼⱼ)` and `|b̂ᵢ − bᵢ| ≤ γₘ√Nᵢᵢ‖r̃‖₂`, which is exactly the form `DEM89` handles. Hence
- **`Θ_s(n) = χₙ [ (3n² + n) u + n³u² ]`** — the factorisation and the two substitutions, in the scaled 2-norm;
- **`Θ_N(n, m) = n γₘ (1 + γₘ) + Θ_s(n)`** — plus the forming of `N`; the test-level versions add `n·η_rep`, `η_rep` the relative representation error of decimal data rounded to double (`2.01 u`, §6.1).
`n` is the number of FREE parameters and `m` the number of rows. **The solve's certified relative error** is `B` of `EST-R-206`.

**3.5 What a solution reports** (`EST-R-207`, `-210`): the condition of `N_s` (an estimate from below and a bound from above), each parameter's own `κᵢ = Nᵢᵢ(N⁻¹)ᵢᵢ = (N_s)ᵢᵢ(Q_s)ᵢᵢ` (unit-free: `GEO1` §10.5), the **weakest direction**, `B`, the residual sum of squares `Ω̂`, the degrees of freedom `ν = m − n_free`, the variance of unit weight `σ̂₀² = Ω̂/ν`, and the covariance **both ways** — formal (`σ₀ = 1`: the weights as stated) and scaled (`σ̂₀²`) — never one for the other (`BRN52` §7.2).

## 4. Required behaviour

*(Written per step; step 2's requirements are `EST-R-201` – `EST-R-213`, then the exit gate's.)*

**Step 2 — the batch estimator, the normal equations scaled by default.**

- **EST-R-201.** **The scale rule, and no path without it.** For a free parameter whose diagonal `d = N̂ᵢᵢ` is finite and lies in `[2⁻¹⁰⁰⁰, 2¹⁰⁰⁰]`, write `d = m·2ᵏ` with `m ∈ [½, 1)` (`std::frexp`); **the scale exponent is `e = ⌊k/2⌋`** (an integer floor division, no logarithm and no tie to break); the scaled diagonal `d·2^(−2e)` lies in `[½, 2)`. `N_s,ᵢⱼ = ldexp(N̂ᵢⱼ, −(eᵢ+eⱼ))`, `b_s,ᵢ = ldexp(b̂ᵢ, −eᵢ)`, the solution `xᵢ = ldexp(yᵢ, −eᵢ)` and the cofactor `Qᵢⱼ = ldexp(Q_s,ᵢⱼ, −(eᵢ+eⱼ))` — **all exact**. The interface offers **no option and no function that factors or solves an unscaled matrix**: unscaled quantities are outputs only. A diagonal `≤ 0` is `EST-F-201`; one non-finite or outside the range, `EST-F-206`.
- **EST-R-202.** **Rows.** `add(row, residual, sigma)` requires `row.size() = n`, every value finite and `sigma` finite and `> 0` (`EST-F-204`; **a weight is never defaulted**: the caller states `σ`, even 1). The row and the residual are whitened by division (`fl(aₖ/σₖ)`, `fl(rₖ/σₖ)`) and stored in the order added.
- **EST-R-203.** **The forming.** `N̂ᵢⱼ` (`i ≥ j`) is the plain double sum `(((0 + p₁) + p₂) + …) + p_m`, `pₖ = fl(ãₖᵢ ãₖⱼ)`, **rows in the order added**; the upper triangle is its mirror image; `b̂ᵢ` likewise with `pₖ = fl(ãₖᵢ r̃ₖ)`. The module is built with fused multiply-add contraction **off**, so that this order and these roundings are the registered ones; the bounds of §3.4 hold with or without contraction.
- **EST-R-204.** **The factorisation and the solves** are `DEM89`'s Algorithm 2.1 on the lower triangle of `N_s` and the two substitutions, in plain double: for `i = 0 … n_f−1`, `t = N_s,ᵢᵢ` minus `Σ_{k<i} L²ᵢₖ` **by successive subtraction in the order `k = 0 … i−1`**; **if `!(t > 0)` the factorisation breaks down at `i`** (`EST-F-202`); `Lᵢᵢ = √t`; for `j > i`, `Lⱼᵢ = (N_s,ⱼᵢ minus Σ_{k<i} LⱼₖLᵢₖ by successive subtraction)/Lᵢᵢ`. Forward substitution `Lz = b_s` ascending, back substitution `Lᵀy = z` descending, each by successive subtraction. Extended precision is **not** used in production.
- **EST-R-205.** **The solution and the cofactor.** `x` by the unscaling of `y`. The cofactor by `n_f` solves `N_s q = eⱼ` with the same factor; `Q_s,ᵢⱼ = qⱼ[i]` for `i ≥ j` and the upper triangle its mirror image, so **`Q` is exactly symmetric**. An eliminated parameter has no uncertainty in this model: its row and column of `Q` are exactly 0.
- **EST-R-206.** **What the solve certifies, and the refusal that follows from it.** Let `q₁ = ‖Q̂_s‖₁` (the largest column sum of absolute values of the computed scaled cofactor), `X = (Σᵢ N_s,ᵢᵢ yᵢ²)^½ = ‖Dx̂‖₂`, `ρ̄ = ‖r̃′‖₂` (the residual vector that formed `b̂`, after any elimination), `ρ̄₀ = ‖r̃‖₂` (before), and `μ_E = ‖(Σ_e |ãₖₑ||vₑ|)ₖ‖₂` (0 when nothing is eliminated). **The certified scaled relative error is**
  **`B = 4 q₁ [ Θ_N(n_f, m) + √n_f (γₘ ρ̄ + γ_{2n_e}(ρ̄₀ + μ_E)) / X ]`** — in system mode (`EST-R-213`) the forming terms are absent and `Θ_N` is replaced by `Θ_s(n_f)`; `B = 0` when `b̂ = 0` (then `x̂ = 0` exactly). *Derivation (informative):* `x̂ − x = N⁻¹(Δb − ΔN x̂)` is an identity for the computed `x̂`; `‖D⁻¹ΔND⁻¹‖₂ ≤ Θ_N` and `‖D⁻¹Δb‖₂ ≤ √n_f(…)` by §3.4; `D(x̂ − x) = A⁻¹D⁻¹(Δb − ΔNx̂)`; `‖A⁻¹‖₂ ≤ 2‖N_s⁻¹‖₂` because `N_s` has its diagonal below 2, and `‖N_s⁻¹‖₂ ≤ 2‖Q̂_s‖₂ ≤ 2q₁` whenever `B < ½` (the computed inverse is the inverse of a matrix within `2Θ_N` of `N_s`, and `2Θ_N q₁ < ¼`). **If `B ≥ ½`, or if `Q̂_s` is not finite, the solve certifies not even one digit and is REFUSED (`EST-F-203`)** — a bound, not a number anyone chose.
- **EST-R-207.** **The condition report.** `κ_est = λ_N λ_Q`, the Rayleigh quotients after **100 power iterations** on `N_s` and on `Q_s`, each started from `(1, …, 1)/√n_f` (so `κ_est ≤ κ₂(N_s)`); `κ_up = ‖N_s‖₁ ‖Q_s‖₁` (so `κ₂(N_s) ≤ κ_up`); **`κᵢ = (N_s)ᵢᵢ (Q_s)ᵢᵢ`** for every free parameter (`NaN` for an eliminated one); **the weakest direction** = the unit vector of the `Q_s` iteration, sign fixed so that its largest-magnitude component is positive, in the scaled variables, 0 for eliminated parameters. These are reported with every solution and with `EST-F-203`.
- **EST-R-208.** **Every refusal names the parameter and why.** `EST-F-201` names every parameter with no information. `EST-F-202` names the parameter `j` at which the factorisation broke down, the pivot `t`, and **the dependency**: with `z` the solution of `N_s[0:j−1, 0:j−1] z = N_s[0:j−1, j]` (the same successive-subtraction substitutions on the factor built so far), `N_s[:, j] ≈ Σ_{k<j} z_k N_s[:, k]` — the three parameters of largest `|z_k|` by name with their `z_k`; the weakest direction reported is `(−z, 1)` normalised, in the scaled variables, 0 for the parameters after `j`. `EST-F-203` carries `B`, `κ_est`, `κ_up`, the three largest `κᵢ` by name and the three largest members of the weakest direction by name.
- **EST-R-209.** **The one hatch: elimination of named parameters at stated values, logged.** `SolveOptions::eliminate` lists parameters by name with a value; each name must be known and listed once, each value finite, at least one parameter must remain and the estimator must hold the rows (`EST-F-204`). The elimination is at the row level: `r̃′ₖ = r̃ₖ − ãₖ,e₁ v₁ − ãₖ,e₂ v₂ − …`, left to right in ascending parameter index, each product and each difference rounded; the normal equations are those of the free parameters and `r̃′`; the solution holds every eliminated parameter at its stated value; `ν = m − n_free`; the provenance records them (`EST-R-212`). This is **the operation a hard constraint is** (step 4), and it removes a parameter from the model: it never returns numbers for combinations the data cannot see.
- **EST-R-210.** **The residual, the degrees of freedom and the covariance.** `ρ̂ₖ = fl(r̃ₖ − sₖ)`, `sₖ = Σᵢ ãₖᵢ xᵢ` over **all** parameters in index order (the eliminated at their values) by successive addition; `Ω̂ = Σₖ ρ̂ₖ²` by successive addition; `ν = m − n_free`; `σ̂₀² = Ω̂/ν` for `ν > 0`. **Formal covariance** `= Q` (`σ₀ = 1`, the weights as stated); **scaled covariance** `= σ̂₀² Q`; standard deviations `√Qᵢᵢ` and `σ̂₀√Qᵢᵢ`; **correlation** `ρᵢⱼ = Q_s,ᵢⱼ / √(Q_s,ᵢᵢ Q_s,ⱼⱼ)` computed from the SCALED cofactor (so it is unit-free by construction), clamped to `[−1, 1]`, `ρᵢᵢ = 1`. `σ̂₀² = 0` (a perfect fit) gives an exactly zero scaled covariance while the formal one stays defined. The two covariances are never substituted for each other.
- **EST-R-211.** **Unit invariance, exactly.** If every column `j` of the rows is multiplied by `2^(pⱼ)` (the parameter `xⱼ` is then `2^(−pⱼ)` of what it was), then — barring over- and underflow — the scale exponents move by `pⱼ`, and **`N_s`, `b_s`, the factor, `y`, `Q_s`, `κ_est`, `κ_up`, the `κᵢ`, the weakest direction, the correlations, `B`, `Ω̂`, `σ̂₀²` and every refusal are bit-identical**; `xⱼ` and the cofactor scale by exactly `2^(−pⱼ)` and `2^(−pᵢ−pⱼ)`. (Every operation commutes with exact power-of-two scalings; `frexp`'s exponent moves by `2pⱼ` and `⌊k/2⌋` by `pⱼ`.) For a rescaling that is not a power of two the solutions agree within the sum of the two certified errors (`EST-A-209`).
- **EST-R-212.** **Provenance.** Every solution carries a string in the tree's `key=value;` form, in this key order: `estimation=batch-normal-equations;scaling=powers-of-two;parameters=<n>;free=<n_free>;observations=<m>;eliminated=<name=value,…|none>;exponents=<e₀,e₁,…>;` — the values printed `%.17g`, the exponents those of the free parameters in order.
- **EST-R-213.** **System mode.** `from_normal_system(names, N, b, observations, residual_square_sum)` takes an accumulated normal system (the lower triangle of `N` is read) for callers who hold one — a combination of campaigns, step 4's priors, a published example. The forming terms of `EST-R-206` are absent (the system is exact as given), `Ω̂ = Σr̃² − bᵀx̂` **only when the caller supplied the residual square sum** (a difference of two large numbers, documented as such), and elimination is refused (`EST-F-204`: it needs the rows).
- **EST-R-900.** *(registered 2026-10-06, before any fit exists.)* The exit gate's fit residual is the statistic **S** of §8.1, computed from the fit's own output by the formula there; a fit **reaches** its frozen residual exactly when `S ≤ F` for its row of §8.1, to the digits `oracle/cases.tsv` prints. The test **prints** `S`, `N`, the per-component and radial/along/cross values and the largest epoch residual beside the frozen value, whichever way the comparison falls.

## 8. Acceptance tests

### 8.1 The exit gate: what "reaches its frozen residual" means — **registered 2026-10-06, before any fit runs** (plan §4 rule 3)

**The frozen rows, as `oracle/cases.tsv` prints them:** `G-01` **0.0645 m** (`validate_sp3.sh 2246 20230220000 G01`), `G-02` **0.0573 m** (`2246 20230230000 G01`), `G-03` **0.1870 m** (`2246 20230220000 G05`), each "GNSS 7-parameter fit residual RMS"; `oracle/ORACLE.md` §6 records them as **cannonball** fits whose seventh parameter is the SRP scale, against IGS final orbits. The stamps are read as the IGS long-name product stamp `YYYY DDD 0000` (GPS week 2246 begins 2023-01-22, day 22): **G01 on 2023-01-22 and on 2023-01-23, G05 on 2023-01-22**.

**The statistic.** For a fit of a 24-hour arc to the IGS file's positions `pᵢ` of the satellite (the file's own epochs `tᵢ` inside the arc, **`N` of them — the 15-minute grid, 96 epochs**; `pᵢ` in the ITRS in metres) with the fitted model's positions `qᵢ(θ̂)` at the same epochs, in the ITRS (the model's GCRS position rotated by the same chain as everywhere in this tree):

> **S = √( (1/N) Σᵢ₌₁..N ‖qᵢ(θ̂) − pᵢ‖² )**, in metres.

**Which components:** all three, as the 3-D Euclidean norm of the position difference at each epoch. **Which epochs:** every epoch of the file's grid inside the arc; **none rejected**, none skipped (an epoch the file marks absent or bad refuses the test rather than being dropped). **Which denominator:** `N`, the number of epochs — **not** `N − 7` and **not** `3N`; no degrees-of-freedom correction. *[v0.2, 2026-10-06, the first text kept: whether the frozen number's own denominator is `N` or `N − 7` is not known and cannot be told from its printed output; `S` with `N − 7` is reported beside `S` with `N`, and the amendment below governs the outcome.]* Reported beside `S`, never instead of it: `S/√3` (the per-component reading), the radial, along-track and cross-track RMS (each `√(mean of that component²)`), the largest epoch residual, `N`, and the fitted parameters.

**How the frozen numbers are used.** They **rank last** (plan §4 rule 2): they are the predecessor's output and detect only gross error — a sign, an axis, a factor of two.
